# Data provenance and licenses

## Track A — synthetic (self-authored)

All Track A corpora are generated by `src/datasets/track_a_synthetic.py` at run
time from invented vocabularies (fictional systems: falconry, kestrel, merlin).
No third-party text. The seed documents in `testdata/` are authored for this
project.

## Track B — QASPER (CC BY 4.0)

Track B uses the QASPER dataset of document-grounded questions over NLP papers:

> Pradeep Dasigi, Kyle Lo, Iz Beltagy, Arman Cohan, Noah A. Smith, Matt
> Gardner. "A Dataset of Information-Seeking Questions and Answers Anchored in
> Research Papers." NAACL 2021. Distributed by the Allen Institute for AI under
> CC BY 4.0: https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/qasper

The dataset is loaded at run time by `src/datasets/track_b_public.py`; the raw
dataset is **not** vendored in this repository. However, the frozen experiment
records under `v18/results_gate0/contexts_B_*.json` and parts of
`v18/results_run/` embed retrieved passages of QASPER paper text, because the
record of what the models were shown is part of the reproducibility claim.
Those passages are redistributed under CC BY 4.0 with attribution as above.
Underlying papers are by their respective authors.

## Model outputs

Files named `replies_*.json`, `answers_*.json` and the `batches/` archives are
verbatim provider responses, retained as the evidence record for the published
figures.
