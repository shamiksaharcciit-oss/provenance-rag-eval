## 1. G2 — ruled: the family is declared over the 170 constructible pairs

The recommendation is adopted, and the distinction the agent flagged holds — here is why,
stated carefully because this programme rejected an exclusion once and the two must never
be confused.

At v1.7's Gate 0, the six straddle queries were *measurable* in every arm; excluding them
would have removed real measurements whose membership was correlated with the mechanism
under test. That is selection. Here, for six queries, **the object `F_SAFE` compares does
not exist for one arm**: the same-doc unanswerable package requires a non-gold unit from
the gold document, and six documents are too small to contain one once gold is excluded.
There is no measurement to exclude — the contrast is undefined there. Redefining a paired
estimand over the population where both sides of the pair are constructible, *declared
before any value exists*, is not selection; it is stating the estimand's domain. The plan
should have censused this and did not — the census family's eighth instance, mine, same
artifact as the seventh (I specified E-A against imagined document sizes with v1.9 §7
sitting in the record naming these exact six documents).

Amendments to freeze:

1. `F_SAFE` is declared over the **170 complete pairs**; the six are listed in the plan
   by id with the construction-impossibility stated per query.
2. **The six `F768`-side packages that do construct are still generated and reported** —
   descriptively, unpaired, in their own table. Six paid-for measurements are not
   discarded because their partners cannot exist.
3. **A scope clause travels with PS-1's result wherever it goes:** the safety finding is
   established on documents large enough to admit the control; the six smallest documents
   are outside its domain. That is a scope note, not a bias — but a reader gets to know
   it.
4. The corpus observation is recorded: the same six short documents have now produced
   three distinct symptoms (v1.9's padding exhaustion, its shortfall table, and this) —
   "minimum document length relative to unit size" enters the candidate file as a corpus-
   design requirement, binding on the future mid-difficulty corpus before it is built.
