## 1. The ruling

For the targeted pair (Track A, `F768` and `U768`, three generation reps each):

- **Each of the three answers is judged exactly once** per answer-level metric — no answer
  is judged twice, no answer goes unjudged. Judge-call totals are unchanged: 15,960,
  matching the frozen projection to the call.
- **B1 is computed per answer, then aggregated.** Reps are paired by index: `F768` rep-r
  with `U768` rep-r, r ∈ {0,1,2}, declared here (the draws are independent, so any fixed
  pairing is exchangeable; index pairing is the one with no discretion in it). For each
  query and each r: the judge-preference indicator on answer-pair r, minus the I2 token-F1
  preference indicator on **the same two answers**. The per-query B1 value is the median
  of the three per-answer differences. `F_BIAS` tests the paired per-query B1 exactly as
  frozen.
- **Descriptive reporting:** I1 answer-level metric values per (query, arm) are the median
  over the three judged answers; I2 is the median F1 over the three answers, exactly as
  PF-3 already said. Context-level metrics stay single-judgement — the contexts are
  byte-identical across reps, and your observation that tripling them would have been pure
  waste is correct and is now in the record.

## 2. Why the other readings fail

**(a)** breaks B1's load-bearing property. B1 is a *between-instrument* comparison: its
entire meaning is "two instruments looked at the same evidence and preferred differently."
Judge one answer while F1 spans three, and the contrast confounds instrument bias with
generation-draw variance — PD-3's "on the identical answers" is not a stylistic phrase, it
is the definition of what makes B1 a bias measurement rather than a noise measurement.
**(b)** restores identical answers by discarding the generation medians PF-3 mandates for
I2, spending 704 generation calls to ignore two-thirds of what they bought. **(d)** — the
only reading that would measure judge variance *separately* — breaches the ceiling, and
separation is not required by any frozen prediction.

Reading (c) with per-answer pairing is not merely the literal fit to PD-3's wording; it is
the statistically correct design. The variance that matters to the tested family is the
variance of the *difference*, and pairing both instruments on the same draw removes
generation variance from it entirely, leaving only judge noise, which the median then
dampens.

## 3. What the judge repeats now measure — PF-3's purpose clause is amended, honestly

PF-3 said the judge repeats measure judge variance. Under this ruling they cover the
**joint** generation-and-judge draw: each judged answer is a fresh generation sample and a
fresh judgement sample, and the two are not separately identified. That is a real
narrowing and it is declared rather than papered over: the results document states that
judge variance and generation variance are deliberately not separated (separation costs
reading (d)'s budget and serves no frozen prediction), and every single-run cell keeps its
existing caveat unchanged.

## 4. The class — a spec collision, and the precedent it sets

This is not the domain-census family. Both clauses were internally coherent; they
conflicted with each other, and the conflict only became visible where the repeats and the
budget met. The precedent, stated narrowly: **when two frozen clauses conflict, the clause
that defines what the tested family measures governs, and machinery clauses bend to it.**
B1's semantics outrank PF-3's variance bookkeeping. A conflict where neither clause is
decision-defining, or where both are, remains a STOP with no default.

Stopping was right, and the specific form deserves note: three readings fit the projection
identically, so the arithmetic could not arbitrate — you caught a semantic fork that every
mechanical check passes. That is the G12 test applied to yourself, before the expensive
stage, with the no-peeking discipline intact. Nothing in this episode needs a candidate
rule beyond the precedent above; the guard that worked was a human-shaped one: asking what
the tested number would *mean* under each reading.
